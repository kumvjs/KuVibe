# Requirement Workflow

Compare `.agents/kuvibe.yaml` with `kuVibe.md` and complete any safe adoption, refresh, or migration first. Then inspect relevant context, normalize the goal, users, behavior, constraints, exclusions, assumptions, and acceptance. Classify completeness as complete, assumable, or incomplete. Ask focused questions only for missing material decisions. Finally classify scope, risk, complexity, and required capabilities.
